How we picked

  • Pulled 3,817 ads from the Meta Ad Library: Explee, 15 AI ad tools (12 run statics), Fastlane AI.
  • Deduped to 833 unique statics, then scored each one.
  • Score: 35% proven (days live, variants scaled), 35% creative (scroll-stop, jaw-drop, action pull, craft), 30% fit for Ad Studio.
  • One clone per format. Dropped anything that needs another brand to work (GTA 6, Drake).

Explee (AI sales agent)

  • One mechanism, same words every time: drop your link, AI finds buyers, books calls.
  • One free offer on every ad: $30 in credits.
  • One disbelief hook, many skins: "I'm sorry, Claude can do WHAT?!" on 12+ surfaces. They test the skin, not the message.
  • Enemy price anchor: Google Ads $2,000/mo vs free. Their longest runner: 83 days, 23 variants (whiteboard).
  • Pain identity: "6 months building, still $0 revenue."
  • Fake native proof (X posts, iMessage, CRM spikes) and physical realism (whiteboard, chalkboard, newspaper, flyer).
  • Ornate print craft (tiles, fruit) to break the SaaS look.

How we adapted Explee

  • Mechanism: paste your product link, AI finds ads that already sell, rebuilds them for you.
  • Offer: your first 3 ads free, on every ad.
  • Hook: "I'm sorry, AI can do WHAT?!" (no Claude, no third-party names).
  • Anchor: ad agency $4,000/mo vs free to start.
  • Pain: "No sales? 6 months live, still $0 from ads."
  • Personas switched from SaaS founders to store owners (candles, soap, skincare, tees).

Competitors

  • Ryze fits us best: owner receipt plus number ladder. "$600 spent. Orders: 0. Then 31 orders in 9 days."
  • Price anchor with a punchline: "$9,000 vs $50. Same output. Different invoice." (Zeely)
  • Objects carry the copy: t-shirt back, burned paper, tally-mark wall, toy blister pack.
  • iOS-native proof: lock-screen orders, Reminders, Notes.
  • Count promise: "One brief. Ten ads by Monday." (Creatify)
  • Pattern interrupts: "We owe everyone a formal apology" (Atria), "Don't try us if you LOVE..." (Creatify).
  • Days-running proof on spied ads (Foreplay). That is our Ad radar signal, so we show the real Ad radar UI.
  • AdCreative.ai and Arcads mostly ran UI cards and video stills. Higgsfield and Magritte run no Meta statics.

Fastlane AI

  • Cinematic hero objects in a dark red glow: racing helmet, sports car, X-ray skull.
  • Black Friday countdown as a question: "32 days away. Do you have enough ads?"
  • Paste URL to "5 ads ready" in one frame.
  • "Six hires or one tool" salary receipt.
  • Woodcut before/after, payment-notification stacks, named testimonials.
  • Short-lived by design (26 days max): themed batches, rotated fast.

How we adapted competitors and Fastlane

  • Their brand colour became Brevo green and mint. Neutrals and concept colours stayed.
  • Their numbers became ours: 3 free ads, $19/mo, 60+ day winners, 3 days to Black Friday.
  • Their products became store-owner products. No "Shopify", USD only.
  • The real Brevo Ad Studio logo is composited in, never drawn by the model.

Inspired sets (B, D, F)

  • Kept each framework's skeleton: one mechanism, one offer, one enemy, specific numbers, a native or physical surface.
  • Changed the surface: wanted poster, police lineup, museum placard, pill bottle, departures board, eye chart, vending machine, parking ticket, fight poster, nutrition label, memes, F1 start lights, thermal camera, card terminal.
  • Timed to BFCM: desk calendar, countdowns, wallet pass, fight night.
  • Every inspired ad lands on the same ask: first 3 ads free.
