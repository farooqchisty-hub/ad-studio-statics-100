What I think

  • Right shape: one ad per ad set, ABO, $10/day, kill fast. It isolates each creative and keeps every ad on equal money.
  • The $8 bar is 4x better than today. Live statics: $31.70 per sign-up (24 on $761). Best static: L17 at $15.11. Videos: $22.23.
  • Two days is enough to kill, not to crown. At $20 per ad, an $8 ad shows 1+ sign-up 92% of the time; a $30 ad only 49%. Kill zero-sign-up ads on day 3, crown on day 5 or 6.
  • Timing: tomorrow is Black Friday week. CPMs spike and store owners are busy. A 1 Dec start reads cleaner; 26 Nov gets the BFCM ads out while they are true.
  • Geo: Spain is your cheapest market ($13.64) and is missing; France is your worst ($35.25) and is in. Australia is untested and outside your NA + EU5 rule.

How it runs

  • Build: CPMA silo as program as_launch4, from this sheet. Batched creates in one call window, everything paused. You check it in Ads Manager, then say Go live.
  • Two additions to the builder: one ad per ad set, and the Advantage+ creative opt-outs (it does not send them today).
  • Controls: L17 ($15.11) and L03 ($15.92) from the live statics run as two extra $10 ad sets, so we know if the new batch beats today's best.
  • Live statics campaign as_launch3 pauses for the test week (same audience, $31.70). The video CBO keeps running as the baseline.
  • Every morning from day 3: CPMA syncs at 09:00 IST, I post one table (spend, sign-ups, cost, CTR, verdict per ad), you say go, I pause in that window.
  • No new ads and no budget edits during the test, except the day 6 winners.
  • Budget: CPMA's November cap is $7,800 and already carries the videos and statics. It needs raising by about $3,000.
